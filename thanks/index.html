Unlock Eagle Commander

  1. Open the e-mail and copy the key — the whole message is fine, Eagle Commander finds the key in it.
  2. In Eagle Commander, choose Help → Licence….
  3. Paste, and choose Unlock.

The same key works on all your Macs. Eagle Commander also keeps a copy of it in ~/Library/Application Support/EagleCommander/Licence.txt.

No e-mail after ten minutes?

Look in the spam folder first. If it is not there, write to support@eaglesoft.si with the order number from Paddle’s receipt, and you will get the key by hand.
