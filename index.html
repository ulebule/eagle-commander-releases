What it does

The panels
  • Two panels with tabs, a folder tree, brief and full listings, quick search by name and a quick filter.
  • Tabs locked to a folder, favourite folders, history, and panels side by side or one above the other.
  • Colour rules by mask, age or size. The branch view: every file below a folder in one flat list.
Files
  • Copy, move and delete with progress, conflicts, cancelling and a background queue.
  • Multi-rename with patterns and counters, attributes and dates, split and join, SHA-256 and BLAKE3 checksums.
  • Compare two folders and synchronize them, with a direction for every pair. Compare two files line by line.
Archives
  • ZIP and TAR read and written, RAR and 7z read. An archive opens like a folder.
  • Encrypted ZIP with AES-256, and the password never on a command line.
Finding things
  • Search by name, size, date and content, as text or a regular expression, inside archives too.
  • Saved searches, and a duplicate finder that decides by content.
Servers and the rest
  • SFTP: a server behaves like a folder, with the same keys as the disk.
  • A viewer for text, hex and images, a command line, and a real terminal below the panels.
  • A button bar, your own commands, and every shortcut rebindable.
  • In English, Slovenian, German, French, Italian and Russian.
Not yet
No plugins (Total Commander’s are Windows libraries), no FTP or WebDAV, no thumbnails. Folders with more than about 20,000 files list noticeably slower.
